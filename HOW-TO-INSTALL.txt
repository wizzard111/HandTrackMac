=====================================================================
  HANDTRACK FOR MAC — STEP BY STEP
=====================================================================

HandTrack recognises hand gestures, can hide your face, lets you draw
in the air and shows all of it in your calls (Zoom, Telegram, Discord,
Google Meet).

You need: macOS 12 Monterey or newer (Apple M1/M2/M3/M4 or Intel),
a webcam, internet during setup, ~1.5 GB of free space.

About the call camera: Apple only allows virtual cameras in apps with
a paid developer signature. So on a Mac HandTrack sends its picture to
the free "OBS Virtual Camera"; the installer offers to install OBS.
OBS itself does not have to stay open.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1. DOWNLOAD AND UNZIP
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Releases page -> handtrack-macos.zip
     (or the green "Code" button -> "Download ZIP").
  2. Double-click the archive: a HandTrack folder appears. It contains:
       Install HandTrack.command     <- the installer
       Uninstall HandTrack.command   <- the uninstaller
       HOW-TO-INSTALL.txt            <- this guide
  The files are already executable.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2. RUN THE INSTALLER
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Double-click  Install HandTrack.command

  2. macOS says it cannot verify the file, because the app is free and
     not signed with Apple. Allow it to run:
       - macOS 15 Sequoia and newer: click "Done", open
         System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
         "Open Anyway", then "Open" again and type your Mac password;
       - macOS 12-14: right-click the file -> "Open" -> "Open".
     This is needed only once.

  3. Terminal opens and shows each step. The installer builds the app
     right on your Mac: 3-6 minutes the first time. If it asks for your
     Mac password, type it (it is not shown while typing; that is
     normal).

  4. "Install OBS now?" — press Enter (= yes).

  5. HandTrack starts and asks for camera access — click "Allow".
     Later, open HandTrack from Applications or Launchpad.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3. TURN THE OBS CAMERA ON ONCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open OBS from Applications and skip the setup wizard.
  2. Bottom right, click "Start Virtual Camera".
  3. macOS asks to allow a system extension:
     System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> "Allow". Then quit OBS.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4. USE IT
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. In HandTrack press "Start" and switch on "Stream to virtual
     camera".
  2. In Zoom / Telegram / Discord choose the camera
     "OBS Virtual Camera".

  * "Hide face" puts a black mask over your face and shows a
    COVERING badge.
  * Drawing: pinch your thumb and index finger together and move
    your hand.
  * "My gestures" with keys: System Settings -> Privacy & Security ->
    Accessibility -> turn on HandTrack.
  * The system-wide Hide face hotkey of the Windows and Linux versions
    is not available on macOS yet.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
UNINSTALL
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Double-click  Uninstall HandTrack.command  (in this folder).
  It removes the app and its permissions and asks about your settings.
  OBS is removed separately: drag it from Applications to the Bin.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Black screen in HandTrack: System Settings -> Privacy & Security
    -> Camera -> turn on HandTrack, then restart it.
  * No "OBS Virtual Camera" in your call: do step 3 and restart the
    call app.
  * Something broke or went missing — just run the installer again.
    It checks what is missing and installs only that, without
    downloading or overwriting anything else.
  * Install log: install.log in this folder.
    App log:     ~/Library/Application Support/HandTrack/logs
